Who we are

About KOFEBD Global Resources

KOFEBD Global Resources is committed to providing a quality and affordable ice cream brand while supporting customers and businesses across Nigeria with products, equipment and practical know-how.

We are the manufacturer of Early Bell Ice Cream and a distributor of Creamier Dela ice cream powder in Nigeria.

We train customers in soft-serve and batch-machine operation, product handling and product costing to help them manage their ice cream businesses with greater confidence.
