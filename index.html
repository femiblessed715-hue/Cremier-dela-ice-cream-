Who We Are

About KOFEBD Global Resources

KOFEBD Global Resources is an ice cream business focused on making quality and affordable ice cream products and business support available to customers across Nigeria.

We are the manufacturer of Early Bell Ice Cream and a distributor of Creamier Dela ice cream powder in Nigeria.

We also help customers learn to operate ice cream machines and understand product handling and costing.

Incorporated: 11 February 2022
