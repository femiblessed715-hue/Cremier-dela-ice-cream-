OUR STORY

More than ice cream. It's an opportunity.

KOFEBD Global Resources is committed to providing quality and affordable ice cream products while helping customers discover opportunities in the ice cream business.

We manufacture Early Bell Ice Cream and distribute Creamier Dela ice cream powder in Nigeria. We also provide practical training to help customers understand ice cream machine operation, product handling and product costing.

Our goal is to make quality products and useful business knowledge accessible to customers and entrepreneurs across Nigeria.
