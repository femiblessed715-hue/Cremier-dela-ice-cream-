About KOFEBD Global Resources

KOFEBD Global Resources was incorporated on 11 February 2022, with our head office at No. 23 Sabon Geri, Abuja, and a regional office at 400 Agbani Road, Enugu. We serve customers across Nigeria.

We manufacture Early Bell Ice Cream and distribute Creamier Dela ice cream powder in Nigeria for businesses making their own soft-serve and batch ice cream. We also train customers to operate their machines and run their businesses confidently.
